Good questions are useful

Before you reach out.

A few straight answers, so the first conversation can stay focused on your system.

What does “parts at cost” mean?

Brimmer passes the part through at the cost paid for it, without adding a parts markup. The expected part cost is discussed before repair work begins.

What is the guaranteed maximum?

It is the maximum labor amount agreed up front for the described repair. If the work takes less time, the labor bill reflects the actual time used; the cap protects you if the job runs long.

Can we start with a video diagnostic?

A free 15-minute video diagnostic is an existing offer. It is a conversation to help decide what the next useful step is, not a promise that a repair can be completed remotely.

How do I know if you serve my address?

Brimmer serves homes within 35 miles of Burlington, WI. Share your town or ZIP code in the contact form and the service area can be confirmed.
